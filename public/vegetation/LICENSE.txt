SIX FORMS
spreading-oak, leaning-linden, multi-stem-birch, low-field-maple,
open-vase-elm, windswept-pine.
The names describe the intended silhouettes, not botanical scans.
Each form has 8 azimuth images and elevation images at 0, 30, 60, 90 degrees.
The four forest silhouettes are compositions of these original whole-tree bakes.
